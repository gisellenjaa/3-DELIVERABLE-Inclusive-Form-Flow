Visible label / question Why necessary now? Required? Native control/type Submitted name/value Autocomplete/inputmode Constraint/instruction Sensitivity and minimization decision
Name In case the institute can set up interviews on the spot. No input type="text" name="name" autocomplete="name" none Low sensitivity: Used mostly to have a list of attendees at the fair, and it helps with employer introductions if there are matching job/interview opportunities.
Student ID To confirm student enrolled status (if not alumni). No input type="text" name="student-id" N/A placeholder attribute (A123456) Sensitive: This information handles all about the student info once the institute looks it up. However it is still optional, because the student email already confirms whether someone was/is a student or not (so pulling up all of the student's personal information won't be needed).
Student email address (required). To be able to submit the form and receive a confirmation. Yes input element with a type="email" attribute. name="email" autocomplete attribute with a value of "email" required attribute, with the addition of a placeholder attribute in the input element to aid the user with a fake email (example: student@school.edu), and with an aria-describedby section. Sensitive: Only the email is required and the information will be handled by the institution as a way to identify the student for registration.
Type of opportunity interested in To see what opportunities can be suggested to the student at the fair. No Three input type="checkbox" describing each opportunity type name="opportunity" (with possible values of part-time, full-time, internship) N/A none Low sensitivity: The institute will use this information to find open opportunities with an employer.
Choose a program: So the institute can find and suggest certain employers at the fair that will fit the student's program No select element with a dropdown list of programs name="program" (with possible values of accounting, graphic-design and psychology) N/A none Low sensitivity: The institute will use this information to find better matches for interview opportunities within their field of study.
Student Status To confirm student enrollment status or previous enrollment, as this fair is an opportunity to current and past students only. It also confirms if they have their degree for certain job opportunities. No two input type="radio" name="student-status" (student/alumni) N/A none Low sensitivity: This is for the institue to confirm the student's status, as only enrolled and past students can participate in the fair. It also may help better matching opportunities as long graduated students could potentially find more non-internship type of jobs.
Graduation Year (2023-2028) For interview opportunities only, as some employers may seek internship immediately or later. No input type="number" with a min and max range for the graduation years name="graduation-year" N/A min value of 2023 and max of 2028 Low sensitivity: The range is to also help determine student/alumni status, and to confirm when they graduate if they are still a student.
I'd like a Career Services appointment For in case a student needs help creating or editing their resume, practice interviews or other career related help they may need. No input type="checkbox" name="want-help" (if checked: value is "help-needed") N/A none Low sensitivity: This checkbox is to confirm whether the student would like additional assitance with career services to prepare for the fair.
Optional Career Services Appointment Space for the student/alumni to describe any help they need to prepare for the fair. No textarea element name="optional-help" N/A maxlength="100", so only a brief message is allowed for the textbox entry. Low sensitivity: Textbox entry is for the student to describe the type of help or assistance they want for the career services appointment. This box is not for sensitive private info.
