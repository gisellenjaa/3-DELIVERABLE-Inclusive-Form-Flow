F1 · Career Services

Job Fair Registration

This form is for current students or alumni that wish to register for the job fair hosted next week. It will take roughly 2-5 minutes to complete.

F1 · Before You Register

Please fill out the form to register. Required fields are marked as (required). Email is required as it is tied to your student account. Name and job type choices will help us find partnered employers for possible interview opportunities on the spot. All your information will be processed through us and is strictly used within the institution.

You will get a confirmation email after submission.

Only email is required. Graduation Year must be between 2023 and 2028. Appointment textarea box has a limit of 100 characters.
